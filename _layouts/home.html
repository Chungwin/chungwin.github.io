How the desk keeps itself honest

Rules first, record in public
01 · Rules

A score moves only for a reason

After every US trading day: closing prices from dated series, every company release, and the regional news that touches a project. A score changes only for news, a price move of 10% or more, or a written re-think, and the reason is recorded on the page. Each Saturday every company is re-checked against its filings.

02 · Scorecard

Every call, then against now

Each company's odds, rank and risk on the day it was first scored, set against today, kept in public whether or not it flatters the method. The record is young: for a 3–5 year horizon it is a ledger, not yet a verdict.

Open the scorecard → 03 · Audit

An independent re-check

After every nightly update, a separate check re-reads exchange filings and newswires, not the company websites the update used, to catch wrong numbers and missed releases. It only reports; the next update fixes, and every correction is listed with its source.
