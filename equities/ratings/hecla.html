Why is there no cheap price?

A share counts as cheap here if, on average, it should be worth at least 2.00 times its price by Nov 2030. Worked out on paper, that would happen at US$6.38 a share. But at that price a share would still be worth US$12.36 if the project stalls, because that value is fixed at today's prices (what the market pays today for this company or for similar stalled projects) and does not fall with the share price. The sum would then say you make money even if everything goes wrong, which is not believable: if the share really fell that far, the value if it stalls would fall with it. So the page shows no cheap price instead of a misleading number.

Why

Fair, on the expensive edge. At US$12.22 (close of Mon 16 Nov 2026) the expected value of a share in four years is 1.08 times today's price (about 1.9% a year). By the numbers that is just under the 1.1× line below which a share counts as expensive, but the desk calls a share expensive only when it can show why the market's assumption will not hold, and here it cannot. The expected value weighs what a share is worth if the plan works and if it fails by their chances. Hecla's two profitable mines, Greens Creek (Alaska) and Lucky Friday (Idaho), earn about US$431M a year at today's silver after their all-in sustaining cost (AISC: the full cost of an ounce, including the spending that keeps the mines going). Valued at 15.0 times that margin, the middle of the three silver-led producers the desk measures, plus four years of free cash flow, a share is worth 1.13 times today's price if the plan works and 1.06 times if not. The plan: Keno Hill's (Yukon) licences amended by the end of 2029 and its mill back at its 2026 rate, and a new pyrite circuit running at Greens Creek, by November 2030. The analyst's chance that it works (a judgement): 30%. For the share to be fair by the numbers the mines need about 15.4 times their margin, about what the best-paid of the other silver-led producers gets, so the evidence does not show that the price is too high.

What would change the rating

Price alone won't make this cheap: below about US$6.38, where the arithmetic would read cheap, the value if the plan fails (US$12.36 a share) is far above the price, and the desk's rule then shows no cheap level (a rule made for projects that may stall; for a producer it is flagged for review). With everything else fixed the cheap line needs silver near US$77. Dates to watch: 2026 production and 2027 guidance (about 26 Jan 2027), Keno Hill's 2027 guidance (February 2027), the pyrite circuit decision (2027), the Keno Hill licence amendments (company: about mid-2029).

Expensive above about US$12.82 (€11.64) with nothing else changed: from there even the high reading of the peer multiple (16.3 times the margin) leaves the expected value under the 1.1× line. By the central numbers alone the line is crossed above about US$11.99. It would also turn expensive at today's price if 2027 guidance (January–February 2027) shows the two mines' AISC at about US$17 an ounce or more (1.00×; 0.95× if their silver is also below 12.5 Moz), and it gets worse if Keno Hill's licence date moves past the end of 2029 or if shares sold under the at-the-market programme (a permission filed on 10 Nov 2026 to sell up to 50 million new shares into the market) pay for something worth less than it cost.

Thesis breakers

Set in advance: any one of these would mean the plan has failed or the rating must be redone.

  • Keno Hill: 2027 guidance below 1.0 Moz of silver, or any company document before 31 Dec 2028 that dates the licence amendments after 31 Dec 2029.

SHARES = 671.777326                 # M, net of treasury, 10-Q cover, 30 Oct 2026

def base_margin(i, ag, au):
    return i['base_oz'] * (ag - i['aisc']) + i['gold_koz'] / 1000 * (au - i['credit_gold_ref'])

def kh_low(i, ag):
    """Keno Hill cash margin a year before capital, throughput held down (estimate)."""
    cost = i['kh_cost_full'] * (i['kh_fixed'] + (1 - i['kh_fixed']) * i['kh_oz_low'] / i['kh_oz_full'])
    return i['kh_oz_low'] * i['kh_rev'] * ag - cost

def kh_full(i, ag):
    """Keno Hill cash margin a year before capital at its Q3 2026 rate."""
    return i['kh_oz_full'] * i['kh_rev'] * ag - i['kh_cost_full']

def pyr_margin(i, ag, au):
    return i['pyr_net'] * (i['pyr_ag'] * ag + i['pyr_au'] / 1000 * au)

def _branches(i, P):
    ag, au, y, t = i['ag'], i['au'], i['years'], i['tax']
    base = base_margin(i, ag, au); khl = kh_low(i, ag); pyr = pyr_margin(i, ag, au)
    khw = max(0.0, kh_full(i, ag) - i['kh_sust'])                      # restored Keno Hill, AISC-style margin
    taxes = t * max(0.0, base + i['sust'] - i['dda'] - i['expl'] + khl + i['interest'])
    fcf_y = base - i['expl'] - i['gc_growth'] + khl - i['kh_capex_low'] + i['interest'] - taxes
    fcf_fail = y * fcf_y
    fcf_works = fcf_fail + i['pyr_years'] * pyr * (1 - t) - i['pyr_capex'] - i['kh_infra']
    cap = max(0.0, (EV_TODAY - i['mult'] * base_margin(i, AG0, AU0)) / KH_AUEQ)   # F1 cap, set at the start close
    kh_stalled = min(i['kh_stalled_per_oz'], cap) * KH_AUEQ
    ev_stalled = i['mult'] * base + kh_stalled
    ev_works = i['mult'] * (base + pyr + khw)
    atm_cash = i['atm_shares'] * P * 0.985 * (1 - i['atm_waste'])
    other = CASH - PREF + i['invest'] + atm_cash
    n = SHARES + AWARDS + y * i['sbc'] / P + i['atm_shares']
    Ms_ps = (ev_stalled + other + fcf_fail) / n
    Mw_ps = (ev_works + other + fcf_works) / n
    Mw_ps = max(Mw_ps, Ms_ps)                                          # works >= fail (brief v0.5)
    wait = i['delay'] * max(0.0, i['kh_capex_low'] - khl) / n          # cash Keno Hill burns during the delay
    Ml_ps = max(Ms_ps, Ms_ps + (Mw_ps - Ms_ps) / R ** i['delay'] - wait)
    Mf_ps = i['q'] * Ml_ps + (1 - i['q']) * Ms_ps
    return dict(base_margin=base, pyr_margin=pyr, kh_works_margin=khw, kh_low_cash=khl - i['kh_capex_low'],
                taxes=taxes, fcf_year=fcf_y, fcf4_fail=fcf_fail, fcf4_works=fcf_works, cap_per_oz=cap,
                kh_stalled=kh_stalled, ev_stalled=ev_stalled, ev_works=ev_works, shares=n,
                Mw_ps=Mw_ps, M_late_ps=Ml_ps, M_stalled_ps=Ms_ps, Mf_ps=Mf_ps)

def value(inp=None):
    i = dict(DEFAULTS, **(inp or {})); P = i['P']; b = _branches(i, P)
    E = (i['pw'] * b['Mw_ps'] + (1 - i['pw']) * b['Mf_ps']) / P
    Eq0 = (i['pw'] * b['Mw_ps'] + (1 - i['pw']) * b['M_stalled_ps']) / P
    return dict(b, Mw=b['Mw_ps'] / P, Mf=b['Mf_ps'] / P, M_late=b['M_late_ps'] / P, M_stalled=b['M_stalled_ps'] / P,
                E=E, E_q0=Eq0, per_year=E ** (1 / i['years']) - 1)

def _E(i, P):
    b = _branches(i, P)
    E = (i['pw'] * b['Mw_ps'] + (1 - i['pw']) * b['Mf_ps']) / P
    Eq0 = (i['pw'] * b['Mw_ps'] + (1 - i['pw']) * b['M_stalled_ps']) / P
    return E, Eq0, b

def _cross(i, line, hi, both=False, lo=0.05, steps=4000):
    import math
    f = (lambda P: min(_E(i, P)[:2])) if both else (lambda P: _E(i, P)[0])
    prev = hi
    for k in range(steps + 1):
        P = hi * math.exp(math.log(lo / hi) * k / steps)
        if f(P) >= line:
            a, b = P, prev
            for _ in range(60):
                mid = (a + b) / 2
                a, b = (mid, b) if f(mid) >= line else (a, mid)
            return a
        prev = P
    return None

def levels(inp=None):
    i = dict(DEFAULTS, **(inp or {})); y = i['years']; hi = 10 * i['P']
    exp_sum = _cross(i, 1.1 ** (y / 4), hi)                              # central sum crosses the expensive line
    exp = _cross(dict(i, mult=i['mult_high']), 1.1 ** (y / 4), hi)       # R1: under the line even at the high multiple
    cheap = _cross(i, 2.0 ** (y / 4), hi, both=True)                     # both E and E at q = 0 must reach the cheap line
    fps = _E(i, cheap)[2]['M_stalled_ps'] if cheap else None
    return dict(expensive_above=exp, expensive_above_sum=exp_sum,
                cheap_below=(cheap if cheap and cheap > fps else None), cheap_arith=cheap, fail_ps_at_cheap=fps)

def label(inp=None):
    """Fair unless the price is above the R1 level (expensive) or a cheap level exists and the price is below it."""
    i = dict(DEFAULTS, **(inp or {})); l = levels(i)
    if l['expensive_above'] is not None and i['P'] > l['expensive_above']: return 'expensive'
    if l['cheap_below'] is not None and i['P'] < l['cheap_below']: return 'cheap'
    return 'fair'

    print({k: round(v, 4) for k, v in r.items()}); print(l); print(label())
