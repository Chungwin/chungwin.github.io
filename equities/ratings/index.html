How a rating works

A rating weighs two futures, the plan working or not, by their chances, and compares the expected value of a share at the end of the horizon (usually four years) with today's price. Expensive is 1.1× or less (about 2.4% a year or less) plus a written reason. Cheap is 2.0× or more (about 19% a year) plus a written reason, a dated catalyst and no tripped thesis breaker. Everything between is fair.
