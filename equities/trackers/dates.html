How to read this page
  • Where it comes from. Plan items are the events written in each entry plan (the same ones the Entry plans tab shows); reporting dates come from each company's Financials tab, where announced means the company published the date and expected means it is estimated from the filing deadline and last year's pattern; Market Temperature readings follow the monthly cadence on that page. Other tracker names adds the same reporting calendar for every company on the trackers without an entry plan; their names open the card.
  • Dates vs windows. A plan rarely has an exact date: most items are windows the company has guided ("Nov–Dec 2026"). A window is listed in the month it opens; once it has opened it moves to Open windows at the top, and once it has closed without a recorded result it moves to Window passed.
  • Tags. Read = a result the plan reads before it buys · Buy = an event that unlocks a buy · Check = a threshold to check · Stop = an event that stops the plan · Results / Production / Annual / AGM = the reporting calendar · Temperature = a dial reading.
  • Links. A company name opens its plan on the Entry plans tab; card ↗ opens the company card on its Entry plan tab.
  • What moves the page. Nothing moves on its own: when a company publishes a date or a result, the plan's event is updated or marked done, and the page is rebuilt nightly.
