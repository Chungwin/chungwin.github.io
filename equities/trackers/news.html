What is listed. Company releases are the companies' own news releases and exchange announcements, linked to the company's site or to the wire or exchange that carried them. "Outside" items are reports by others (press, regulators, partners), labelled with the outlet; until a company confirms them they are reports, not facts. The one-line notes are the research's own read and are judgement, not data.

Entry plan marks a release that touches a point written in advance in the company's entry plan (a test with a green and a red line, a trigger, a stop, a date). Each line sets what the plan said beside what the release says and the verdict: Green or Red against the plan's own lines, Between the lines, Watch (a stop or override touched but not triggered) or Not decided yet (the release bears on a step whose deciding numbers are not out yet, e.g. a date kept or progress toward a study). The verdicts are the research's read against thresholds set beforehand, not advice.

Odds changes are the estimated chance that a share price at least triples within 3–5 years, as set in the rankings on that day, with the event that explains the change. Many changes follow the share price alone: a lower price with the same outlook means better odds of a 3× from here.
