How this page works
  • One source. Each card keeps its full plan in its Entry plan & Watch tab. This page reads a short summary of each plan, and the nightly update recomputes it with the latest prices.
  • "Buy level reached" and "Results due" are automatic. The first lists tranches whose buy price has been reached (and price alerts hit). The second lists results due within two weeks (a week before an exact date) or overdue.
  • Steps move only when a buy is recorded. Nothing moves on its own.
  • Buying in EUR. Each limit is the home-exchange limit converted at the ECB rate shown above, so the EUR figure moves with the exchange rate even when the plan has not changed. Before ordering on a German venue, compare its bid and ask with the converted home price; on thin names the spread can be 2–5%.
  • Fees. At about €4 an order, keep each tranche at €200 or more so the fee stays at 2% or less; if a tranche would come out smaller, merge it with the next one.

Human judgement on what matters. Machine effort on everything else. I set the rules; Claude, an AI by Anthropic, does the homework every night.
