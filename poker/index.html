Before you sit down

The Playbook was written for one job: beating NL25 6-max on GGPoker, one of the busiest games online. Don't let the stakes fool you. A regular winner at NL25 is better than most people who play poker — online, and almost certainly at the pub.

The Playbook and this trainer were built with Claude (mostly Fable 5.1), and they're built from the same data: the charts, the flop lists and the reasons are one file, so what the trainer marks wrong is exactly what the Playbook says — with a link to the page.

It's a plan for the players who actually sit at those online tables — the ones who limp, call too much, and never bluff the river — rather than a solver's idea of poker. Where the Playbook departs from theory, it tells you why.

Preflop covers every seat and every chart. The flop covers the spots you'll meet most: c-betting, defending against a c-bet, iso pots, multiway, and 3-bet pots from both sides.

Two house rules. The wrong bet size is a miss — size is half the decision. And the turn and river stay in the Playbook: they're principles, and principles don't deal cards.
